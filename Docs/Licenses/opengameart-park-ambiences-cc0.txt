The source page describes three field recordings made in a public park in Adelaide,
South Australia, in winter. All are 48 kHz stereo PCM: birds near trees before rain,
a small river with bird and frog calls, and stronger wind in an open field. The
wind recording is 24-bit; the other two are 16-bit. Birds and wind are used whole.
The river recording download was unusually slow, so the game uses the first 130.204
seconds. Its audio samples are unchanged in the archived excerpt WAV; the imported
loop has a 2-second crossfade at its tail and is 128.204 seconds long.

Birds and wind source WAV files are preserved byte-for-byte in:
  Docs/AssetArchives/park-ambiences-cc0/
The river source excerpt is preserved in `park_ambience_river_source_excerpt.wav` in
that directory; the looped version is `Assets/Resources/Audio/Ambience/park_ambience_river.wav`.
Recreate the crossfaded loop with `python3 Tools/make_park_river_loop.py`.
Unity uses PCM, streaming load and the preserved 48 kHz sample rate for Android.
